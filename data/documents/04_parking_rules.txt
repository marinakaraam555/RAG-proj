Parking Regulations

Parking is prohibited within 5 meters of a fire hydrant, 10 meters of an intersection corner, and within any marked crosswalk or bicycle lane. Vehicles parked in these zones are subject to immediate towing at the owner's expense, in addition to a standard fine.

Disabled parking spaces require a valid, visibly displayed disability permit issued to the vehicle occupant. Using a disability space without a valid permit, or displaying a permit not issued to the current occupant, carries a significantly higher fine than an ordinary parking violation and may result in permit revocation for the actual permit holder if misuse is repeated.

Time-limited parking zones (indicated by posted signage, e.g. "2-hour parking 8 AM-6 PM") require the vehicle to move to a different block or zone once the limit expires; simply re-feeding a meter in the same spot without moving the vehicle does not reset the clock in jurisdictions with chalk-marking or camera-based dwell-time enforcement.

Overnight parking restrictions apply on many residential streets to allow street cleaning, typically one designated night per week per side of the street. Vehicles left in violation are ticketed and, if street cleaning is obstructed, towed to a municipal impound lot.

Double parking (stopping alongside another parked vehicle, blocking a travel lane) is prohibited except for active loading or unloading not exceeding a few minutes, and never when it blocks a bus lane, bike lane, or emergency access route.
